Invite a friend

Everyone with active premium has a friend code. We reward you with days of premium, not money.

  1. 01Find your code

    In ESHArio open the settings and the “Account and help” section. Your code is in the “Invite a friend” block.

  2. 02Send it to a friend

    Copy the code or the link with it. With the link the code appears on the purchase page by itself.

  3. 03Get your days

    Your friend pays less, and your days of premium arrive right after their payment.

How it works

  • For the first purchase of each of your first five new clients we pay one and a half times as much. One counter per partner
  • A client is tied to you on their first payment with your code for the whole time of our partnership; every renewal of theirs brings you the same payout without the code
  • The code discount is for the first purchase only
  • Payout on request from 3,000 ₽, within 1 or 2 days
  • Hold 14 days, a refund cancels the accrual
  • Your personal link shows how many clients you brought, how much you earned and how much is due
  • While you advertise us, your premium is free
  • We give you ready text for ads
  • Label your ads the way your country’s law requires
  • No exclusivity needed. We do not promise income

How to become a partner

  2. 02Get your code

    We send your personal code and free premium.

  3. 03Share the link

    Give viewers the link with your code. The discount fills in on the purchase page by itself.
