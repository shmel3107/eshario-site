Partner programme terms

These terms apply to everyone we have issued an ESHArio promo code to. The programme is run by the ESHArio administration. Full details on request at support@eshario.com. By advertising the product with our code you accept these terms.

1. Who a partner is

A partner is a person or company we have issued a promo code to.

A partner acts in their own name. They are not our agent, employee or representative, and may not make promises on our behalf.

2. What we pay

For every payment with the partner’s code we pay a fixed sum in rubles, not a percentage:

  • 1 month: 300 ₽
  • 3 months: 800 ₽
  • 12 months: 3,000 ₽

For the first purchase of each of the partner’s first ten new clients the sum is one and a half times that: 450, 1,200 and 4,500 ₽. Renewals are paid at the normal rate.

A new client is an email that has not paid for access before.

3. How it counts

An accrual arises when the payment has gone through and the code was entered on the buy page. The accrual then waits 10 days (the hold).

A refund to the buyer cancels the accrual.

Accruals are not paid for payments from the partner’s own or relatives’ emails, or for payments the payment partner deems fraudulent.

4. Payouts

On the 1st and the 16th, from 1,000 ₽, by the method agreed by email.

The partner pays taxes on the reward under the law of their country.

5. Premium for partners

While the partner advertises the product (clients come or a banner stands), premium is free for them.

When advertising stops, free premium ends with the current term.

6. Materials

The partner describes the product in the words of our showcase, and we provide ready text. The partner does not promise results in the game and does not present ESHArio as an EA product or as affiliated with EA.

We may ask to remove or correct a material.

7. Advertising law

The partner complies with the advertising law of their country. In Russia that is the «Реклама» label and the erid token via an advertising data operator, registry listing for channels with 10,000 or more subscribers, and no advertising on platforms whose access is restricted in Russia.

Fines for a breach are the partner’s own.

8. Prohibited

  • Spam
  • Self-purchases and invented clients
  • Misleading buyers
  • Buying search ads on our name
  • Posting the code on coupon sites without our consent

9. Termination

Either side may end participation by letter. Accruals past the hold are paid.

On a breach of clauses 6, 7 and 8 (materials, advertising, prohibitions) we may cancel unpaid accruals and the code.

10. Changes

A new version is published on this page with a date. Payments made before it keep the earlier terms.

Disputes are settled by letter to support@eshario.com first. If that does not work, the dispute is resolved in court under the applicable law.
