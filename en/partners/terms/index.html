Partner programme terms

who a partner is, what and how much we pay, what must not be said in ads

Version25 November 2026SellerThe ESHArio administrationContactsupport@eshario.com

These terms apply to everyone we have issued an ESHArio promo code to. The programme is run by the ESHArio administration. Full details on request at support@eshario.com. By advertising the product with our code you accept these terms.

1. Who a partner is

A partner is a person or company we have issued a promo code to.

A partner acts in their own name. They are not our agent, employee or representative, and may not make promises on our behalf.

Taking part in the programme does not create employment, a partnership or a joint venture. The partner decides when, where and how to talk about the product and bears their own costs.

2. What we pay

For the first payment with the partner’s code and for every renewal by a client tied to the partner we pay a fixed sum in rubles, not a percentage:

  • 1 month: 300 ₽
  • 3 months: 800 ₽
  • 12 months: 3,000 ₽

For the first purchase of each of the partner’s first five new clients the sum is one and a half times that: 450, 1,200 and 4,500 ₽. Renewals are paid at the normal rate.

A new client is an email that has not paid for access before.

3. How it counts

An accrual arises when the payment has gone through. A client is tied to the partner on their first payment with the partner’s code for the whole time of our partnership; every renewal by the client brings the partner the same payout without the code. The accrual then waits 14 days (the hold).

The code discount is for the first purchase only. An email that has already paid gets no code discount and is not tied to the partner.

A refund to the buyer cancels the accrual. A refund of the first payment also unties the client.

Accruals are not paid for payments from the partner’s own or relatives’ emails, or for payments the payment partner deems fraudulent.

4. Payouts

On the partner’s request, when at least 3,000 ₽ is due, within 1 or 2 days, by the method agreed by email.

The partner sees clients, earnings and the sum due in their account on the site after signing in.

The partner pays taxes and contributions on their reward under the law of their country. If a document is needed for the payout, such as a self-employed receipt, an invoice or a statement, the partner sends it before the payout.

5. Premium for partners

While the partner advertises the product (clients come or a banner stands), premium is free for them.

When advertising stops, free premium ends with the current term.

6. Materials

The partner describes the product in the words of our showcase, and we provide ready text. The partner does not promise results in the game and does not present ESHArio as an EA product or as affiliated with EA.

We may ask to remove or correct a material.

7. Advertising law

The partner complies with the advertising law of their country. In Russia that is the «Реклама» label and the erid token via an advertising data operator, registry listing for channels with 10,000 or more subscribers, and no advertising on platforms whose access is restricted in Russia.

The advertiser for ESHArio advertising is the seller of the service, a foreign person. We give the partner the advertiser’s details for the advertising data operator and for the “Реклама” label by email on request. The partner registers the advertising and gets the erid themselves or through their platform, unless we have agreed otherwise by email.

Fines for a breach are the partner’s own.

8. Prohibited

  • Spam
  • Self-purchases and invented clients
  • Misleading buyers
  • Buying search ads on our name
  • Posting the code on coupon sites without our consent

9. Termination

Either side may end participation by letter. After termination the code no longer works, tied clients are released, and accruals past the hold are paid.

On a breach of clauses 6, 7 and 8 (materials, advertising, prohibitions) we may cancel unpaid accruals and the code.

10. Changes

A new version is published on this page with a date. Payments made before it keep the earlier terms.

Disputes are settled by letter to support@eshario.com first. If that does not work, the dispute is resolved in court under the applicable law.
