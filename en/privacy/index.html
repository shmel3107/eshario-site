ESHArio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026. This page describes the ESHArio browser extension for Google Chrome, the eshario.com website and the purchase of a key.

Short version

ESHArio keeps your data on your own computer. It leaves only by your own action: your email and password to our server when you sign in to an account; your settings to our server when you press "Save settings"; a problem report when you press "Send". Without such a press, without signing in and without asking us to reset your password, the extension sends nothing about you anywhere. It never sees your EA account password. There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising in the extension or on the site; on the purchase and order pages only the payment partner’s verification code runs. We do not sell your data and do not share it for anyone else's advertising. To run the service, some of the data is handled by our suppliers: the payment partner, hosting and a mail service. When you buy, we receive your email and the order details from the payment partner (see "The website and your purchase"). We occasionally email buyers about discounts; you can opt out with the link in any such email.

What ESHArio is

ESHArio is an extension for Google Chrome that adds tools inside the official EA SPORTS FC Web App at https://www.ea.com/*/ultimate-team/web-app*. It runs on that address and nowhere else. On every other page it does nothing at all: no script is injected and no request is made.

ESHArio is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Electronic Arts Inc.

What is stored on your device

ESHArio uses Chrome's storage permission to keep its own data in your browser's local storage. The data consists of:

  • Your settings. Which features are on, hotkey assignments, display choices, and which panels are open.
  • Your saved filter sets. The market searches you chose to save.
  • A local copy of your club list. Card identifiers, ratings and similar game attributes, so squads can be built without asking EA for the same list again. It holds nothing about you as a person.
  • A purchase log. The price you paid for a card: EA discards that number itself.
  • Action counters. How many market searches, purchases and challenge submissions were made in the last hour and day; the extension keeps them for its own operation.
  • Reference data about game cards. A catalogue of card definitions and rarity groups: part of it is read from the Web App itself, part is downloaded from our server (see "What is sent over the network"). It holds nothing about you.
  • The contents of a pack preview you have opened. The card identifiers from that preview, their quick-sell value and whether they can be traded, with no market prices. The record lives until EA changes what that preview holds, and it never travels to the server.
  • A licence key, if you entered one. It is signed, verified on your own machine, never sent anywhere, and says nothing about you.

Chrome stores it on the machine where the extension is installed. The exceptions are described below under "Moving your settings to another computer" and "Problem reports", and you switch them on yourself.

Moving your settings to another computer

You can move your settings to another computer through your ESHArio account with two buttons. "Save settings" puts the settings of this computer on our server in place of the previous copy. "Load settings" replaces the settings of this computer with the copy from the server. The buttons are in the extension's settings window (the "Account and help" section) and on the account page; nothing travels without a press. If the server holds a copy saved from another computer after your last one, "Save settings" tells you so first and replaces it only on a second press.

What travels: feature toggles, saved filter sets, buying and selling settings, the listing and relisting price rules, how pack openings are shown, sound volume, squad builder settings, hotkeys, the pick preselection and pack locks. What never travels: your EA login and password, the licence key, the local club mirror, card identifiers, the purchase log and the action counters.

The copy lives in our database on a rented server in Germany, and only we have access to it. The connection is encrypted (HTTPS); the settings themselves are not encrypted in the database, and neither is the account email. Seven daily backups of the database are kept on the same server. The settings copy is deleted together with the account at your request.

The extension no longer uses Chrome's own cloud for settings: it neither writes there nor reads from there. If you switched on the transfer through Chrome's cloud before, the old copy stays in your Google account until you remove the extension on every computer or clear its data in Chrome.

What is sent over the network

ESHArio makes network requests to exactly two third-party sites (four host patterns in the manifest), and only to read public reference data. Both sites, r2.fut.gg and www.fut.gg, belong to the fut.gg price service: from there the extension takes the shared market price file and the public list of Squad Building Challenges with community ratings. What leaves your computer is only a page number and the id of the challenge you opened. The requests go without cookies, and fut.gg does not know who asked.

The fourth pattern is not a third party at all: it is our own server, api.eshario.com. The extension contacts it on three separate errands, and a copy installed without the store on a fourth one.

Your account. When you sign in yourself, ask to reset a password or press "Save settings" or "Load settings"; and while you are signed in it also asks that server from time to time whether your paid access is open: when the web app opens, when you come back to it and when you press "Refresh". What goes there is described under "Accounts and paid access".

A problem report. When you press "Send" yourself in the "Report a problem" window. What a report holds and what it never holds is described under "Problem reports".

The card reference. The extension downloads a card reference from our server: the rating, position, nation, league, club and rarity of the season's game cards. That is data about game cards only; nothing about you is sent there. The download happens on its own, a few times a day, and works without an account: the session token takes no part in that request, no cookies are set, and we do not record who downloaded the file.

The latest version number. A copy of the extension installed from our website without the store asks the server every few hours for the number of the latest version and the address of the download page, so it can tell you that a newer version is out. That request carries no session token, no cookies and nothing about you. A copy from the Chrome Web Store never makes it: the store itself keeps that copy up to date.

ESHArio has no analytics and no telemetry. There is no usage reporting, no crash reporting that sends itself and no advertising network: a problem report leaves only when you press the button. Requests to EA's servers are only the ones the web app itself makes with your own EA session; they carry no identifier of ours.

What ESHArio never touches

  • Your EA credentials. ESHArio does not read, store, transmit or fill in your EA email address, your password, or your two-factor code. You sign in yourself, in EA's own form. The only thing ESHArio presses on its own is the platform choice after you are already signed in, which saves one click.
  • Your cookies and your EA session. ESHArio does not read cookies. It does not request the cookies permission and cannot.
  • Your browsing history. ESHArio does not request the history or tabs permission and has no way to see what other pages you visit.
  • Other websites. ESHArio has no permission to run on any site other than the EA Web App and the two read-only sites above, plus our own server api.eshario.com, which it also writes to: sign-in, access rights, password recovery, the settings and the problem report you send by pressing a button, and from which it downloads the card reference and the latest version number.
  • Your clipboard's contents. ESHArio can place a problem report into your clipboard when you press "Copy report". It never reads what is already there.

Accounts and paid access

ESHArio is a free set of features plus paid access for a term, opened either in an account or by a licence key. You sign in yourself, inside the extension and never on an EA page: your email and password go to our server api.eshario.com. Along with them go a random device name the extension invents for itself, which is tied to nothing but our own extension, and a short device label such as "Windows · Google Chrome": the "new device" email names it, and the limit on devices per account counts it. No other information about you is in these requests. What comes back is a signed statement of your paid access and a session token; later requests to the server carry that token, while signing in itself and password recovery go without it.

The server holds: your email address, an irreversible hash of your password (never the password itself), device sessions, orders, an account event journal (a sign-in, a sign-out, a renewal) and a mark that you opted out of offer emails. A session is limited in time and in the number of devices. The "set your password" link from the email works for 24 hours and once. The IP address is used to restrict how often sign-in, orders and card-reference downloads can be attempted and is not stored in the database; an order keeps only a hash of it, from which the address itself cannot be read (see "The website and your purchase").

On your computer the extension keeps a signed snapshot of the access (it keeps the extension working offline for up to 7 days) and the session token. The session token goes to our account server only; the EA page and any other website cannot reach it.

The extension's settings go to our server only if you press "Save settings" yourself (see "Moving your settings to another computer"). Club lists, purchase journals, the action counters and the licence key never travel.

Problem reports

The extension's settings window has a "Report a problem" button in the "Account and help" section. In its window you write a few words about what happened and choose what to do with the report yourself:

  • "Send": the report goes to our server, and we receive a short summary of it in our Telegram. It leaves only when you press the button.
  • "Copy report": the report is placed in your clipboard, and you decide whom to send it to.
  • "Write to support": our support Telegram opens; what to write is up to you.

What a report holds: your words; your Telegram for a reply, if you entered it (the field is shown only to people who are not signed in); the extension version, browser, system, window size and language; which Web App screen is open and which of our additions are on it; the extension's errors during this session and its recent events; your settings (the same set as "Save settings" carries); your plan and the days left. If you are signed in, the server links the report to your account itself and adds the account email to the summary we receive in Telegram, so that we can answer you.

What a report never holds: your EA login and password, your ESHArio password and session token, the access snapshot, the licence key, your email, your club list, card identifiers, the purchase log and screenshots. No email leaves your computer inside the report either: anything that looks like an email address or a key is removed before sending and once more on the server; the account email of a signed-in person comes from our own database.

A report is kept in our database on the rented server for 90 days and then erased; it is deleted together with the account. The device number and the network address are kept next to the report only as hashes, from which the values themselves cannot be read: they are needed to limit how often reports can be sent. The Telegram summary carries an email only if you are signed in. Messages in Telegram are kept by Telegram under its own rules.

The website and your purchase

Our website eshario.com sets no cookies. It has three forms. The purchase form: the email, the chosen term, the promo code and the page language go to our server when you press "Pay"; for the time of payment the page keeps the order number in this browser tab's storage, to show it on the "Your order" page. The promo code field: the code you enter is kept in your browser's storage so you do not have to type it again, and is sent to our server when you press "Apply" (to show the price; the buy page checks a saved code the same way by itself) and "Pay"; nowhere else. And the new-password form on the /password page, which the link in our email opens: the password you type goes once to our account server and nowhere else. The site is served through a hosting provider; like any host, it keeps technical logs with visitors' IP addresses, they live with the host under its rules, and we do not read them. The visit counter, when enabled, works without cookies and without identifying the visitor; it is not on the purchase and order pages.

Payment. The payment is processed on the side of one of our payment partners. On the purchase page and on the order page the code of one of them runs: it matches your email and the technical data of the session (browser, device, network address) against the payment, so that the payment goes through correctly and safely; sometimes the partner may ask you to pass an additional check before paying. The partner processes this data under its own policy, and what its code keeps in your browser is also up to the partner. From the partner that took the payment we receive order data: the number, the amount, the date, the payment status and the payment method; one of the partners also sends the email given at checkout. We never see card details.

Emails about the key, the account and our offers are sent by a mail service from noreply@eshario.com; it sees the address and the text of the email. Order and account records are kept on our rented server; only we have access to it. Support emails arrive through email forwarding into our mailbox; Telegram conversations are stored by Telegram under its rules. Our hosting and mail suppliers process data on our instructions.

What we keep about a purchase, and why:

  • Email and order number: to deliver the key and answer emails.
  • Amount, term, promo code: refunds and the partner's reward.
  • Order records: tax accounting.
  • Email: we may send buyers an email about new offers and discounts; you can opt out with the link in any such email or through support.
  • Support correspondence: to help you.
  • Delivery log, the record of consent to the documents, the network address hash and correspondence in a dispute: defence against payment disputes and abuse.

We process purchase data to perform our contract with you and to meet legal requirements; offer emails to buyers are sent in our legitimate interest until you opt out. Our suppliers (hosting, mail, the payment partner) may be located in other countries and work under contracts with us.

Order records are kept as long as tax accounting requires; an account lives until you ask us to delete it. Your rights: to learn what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, and to object to its processing. The partner whose promo code you entered does not see your data: only the fact of payment and the term.

Sharing and selling

ESHArio does not sell your data, does not share it for anyone else's advertising, and does not use it to assess creditworthiness or for lending. Purchase data is used only for delivery, support, refunds, tax records and emails to buyers about our offers (you can opt out of them), and is passed only to the suppliers named in the section "The website and your purchase", in the amount their work needs.

The use of information received through ESHArio adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Data stays in your browser until you remove it. To delete all of it, remove the extension from chrome://extensions. Chrome deletes the extension's local storage with it. Removing the extension deletes nothing in your EA account. Retention for purchase and account data is set out in the section "The website and your purchase".

Children

ESHArio is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them. Apart from the account email, the purchase data and a problem report a person sends themselves, the extension collects no personal data from anyone; access may be bought by a person with legal capacity, and children may use the extension only with parental consent.

Changes to this policy

If ESHArio's handling of data changes, this page is updated first, with a new date at the top, and the Chrome Web Store listing is updated at the same time.
