How payment works

Methods
The payment page shows the payment methods. The set depends on the payment partner.
Currency
Rubles (₽). The final amount is shown on the payment page before you confirm.
Access
Right after payment, an email with a link to set your password; you sign in from the ESHArio icon in Chrome (email and password). A licence key works too.
The email
Order number, the link to set your password, links to the offer and the refund policy.
Renewal
By a new purchase; the remaining days are added.

After payment

Install the extension
From the Chrome Web Store, free and in one step.
Sign in
Press the ESHArio icon in the Chrome toolbar, the sign-in window opens, enter your email and password. A key is accepted too.
Not working
Write to support with your order number. If it is our fault and we cannot fix it within three working days, we refund the unused days. All terms are in the refund policy.

A new key; remaining days are added.
