Questions

Your question is not here? Ask on the support page.

How do I install it?

From the Chrome Web Store in one step: the «Install» link in the header. Then open the web app at ea.com and ESHArio appears in the top bar by itself.

Which payment methods?

In rubles; the payment page will show the methods, and it is being connected. Until it opens, a key can be obtained from the buy page.

Which platforms are supported?

All the ones the web app opens for: PlayStation, Xbox and PC. The extension works with your account, not with a console.

Which devices are supported?

Anything that runs Chrome: Windows, Mac and Linux. The extension lives in the browser, not in the system. Phones will not do.

How often do updates ship?

The extension updates itself through Chrome. We are actively building it and will keep supporting it: improvements ship when ready.

The key does not work

Paste it whole, with no spaces, and then update the extension at chrome://extensions. Still stuck: write to support with your order number and we will help.
