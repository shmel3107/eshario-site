How do I install it?

Download the archive on the install page and follow the steps with pictures. ESHArio appears in the web app by itself. iPhone and Android have their own steps.

Where does it work?

On a computer in Google Chrome (Windows, Mac or Linux), on iPhone in Safari with Userscripts and on Android in Firefox with Violentmonkey, steps on the install page. Any web app account works, from PlayStation, Xbox or PC.

How do I pay?

In rubles or US dollars, as a one-off payment. SBP, Mir card, cryptocurrency, Visa / Mastercard, Apple Pay / Google Pay or PayPal, on the purchase page.

How fast does access arrive?

By email right after payment. The email has the order number and a link to set your password.

Will I be charged every month?

No, it is a one-time payment. No auto-renewal. When the term ends, renew yourself if you want to.

Is there a free version?

No. Without a purchase the extension shows only the account sign-in, after a purchase everything is open. One plan, you only pick the term.
