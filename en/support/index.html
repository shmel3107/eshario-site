Frequent questions

The key does not work

Paste it whole, no spaces. Update the extension on chrome://extensions. Still stuck: write to us.

The key expired

A key lives 30, 90 or 365 days from issue. Renewal is a new key.

Lost the key

Write to support from the email you used at checkout. We reissue it.

New computer

Install the extension and enter the key again. It is in the email after payment.

Refund

The key fails through our fault, a payment went through twice, or you changed your mind within 14 days without activating the key. Details in the refund policy.
