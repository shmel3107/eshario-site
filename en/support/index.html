Frequent questions

I cannot sign in

Sign in with the email and password from your purchase email. Forgot the password? Press “Forgot password” in the sign-in window and a link arrives by email. Still stuck? Write to us.

The email did not arrive

Check the Spam and Promotions folders. No email after 10 minutes? Write to us with the order number and the email you paid with.

How to pay

On the purchase page pick a term and a payment method. SBP, Mir card, cryptocurrency, Visa / Mastercard, Apple Pay / Google Pay or PayPal. Access arrives by email right after payment.

Will I be charged every month

No, it is a one-time payment. No auto-renewal. When the term ends, renew yourself if you want to.

My term ended

Premium lasts 30, 90 or 365 days from issue. When the term ends, the extension and your settings stay in place, and everything works again after you renew on the purchase page. Renew early and the days left are added.

Another computer

Install ESHArio from the install page and sign in with the same email and password. Premium works on one computer and one phone at a time, a second computer is paused. How to get a second seat is on the “One computer at a time” page.

Works now and then

Turn off other FUT extensions at chrome://extensions and reload the Web App page. Two helpers on one screen get in each other’s way. Still broken: write to us.

Refund

The key fails through our fault, a payment went through twice, or you changed your mind within 14 days without activating the key. Details in the refund policy.

Can I share my account

No. An account is for one person. The server sees how many seats and addresses it works from, and when it is shared, access is closed.

Is ESHArio connected to EA

No. ESHArio is a third-party tool: your relationship with EA remains between you and EA, and the decision to use ESHArio on your account is yours (section 5 of the terms).

Is there a free version

No. Without a purchase the extension shows only the account sign-in. After a purchase the whole extension is open; there are no separate tiers.
