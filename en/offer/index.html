1. Words used here

  • Extension: ESHArio, an extension for Google Chrome that works inside EA’s official web app.
  • Premium features: the extension’s features that paid access unlocks. The list is on the buy page and in the Chrome Web Store listing.
  • Premium access: the right to use the premium features for the term. It is opened in an account we create for the email given at checkout, or by a licence key.
  • Account: an email and a password. You sign in inside the extension itself, never on an EA page.
  • Key: a string of characters you enter in the extension. The key is signed by us and verified on your computer.
  • Term: 30, 90 or 365 days from the moment access is issued.
  • You: the person who paid for the key. A key may be bought by an adult. For anyone under 18, a parent or another legal representative makes the purchase in their own name.

2. What we sell

A simple (non-exclusive) licence: the right to use the extension’s premium features for the term, on your own computers, for yourself. The extension is a tool: everything it does in EA’s web app happens at your instruction, in your name and on your account.

We do not sell game coins, EA accounts, cards or any other in-game items. We do not promise results in the game: how many coins you earn and how many challenges you complete depends on you and on the market.

The extension itself is free. Six of its features work without a key: one-step sign-in, EA’s tax calculator, our top bar with counters, keyboard navigation, closing EA’s announcement pop-ups and search in EA lists.

3. How the contract is made

You choose a term on the buy page, enter a promo code if you have one, tick the consent boxes for immediate key delivery and pay on the payment partner’s page.

The contract is made the moment the payment goes through: paying means you accept the offer in full. Nothing needs to be signed: the contract in electronic form has the force of a written one.

Payment is taken by the payment partner in rubles; the payment methods are shown on the payment page. We never see or store your card details.

4. Prices and terms

  • 1 month (30 days): 1,149 ₽.
  • 3 months (90 days): 3,149 ₽.
  • 12 months (365 days): 10,999 ₽.

Prices are in rubles and apply at the moment of payment. If your bank charges you in another currency, the bank sets the rate. We may change prices; keys already paid for are not affected.

A promo code gives a discount off the price without a code. The size of the discount is shown when you enter the code on the buy page and on the payment page. One promo code per payment.

5. Access delivery and term

Right after payment we create an account for the email given at checkout and send a link to that address for you to set your password. The link works for 24 hours; a new one can be requested inside the extension with the “Forgot your password” button.

A licence key still works as a fallback: on your request, and while the payment page is not connected yet. It arrives by email at the same address and is entered in the extension.

Account emails: access issued, a sign-in from a new device, a password change. They carry no advertising.

The term is counted from the moment access is issued, not from the first sign-in.

There is no auto-renewal. Renewal is a new payment. Payment is one-off; there are no repeat charges. If the previous term is still running, its remainder is added to the new one.

If the extension did not work with EA’s web app for longer than a day after a version change of the web app, those days are added to the term.

6. What you may and may not do

You may: sign in and enter the key on your own computers, change computer and browser, use every premium feature during the term.

One account works on two devices at a time. Signing in on a third signs out the device that has been idle longest, and we tell you by email. Keep your password to yourself and do not pass it on.

You may not: transfer, sell or publish the key or give it to other people; modify the extension’s code or bypass the key check or the built-in limits; use the extension to sell coins, accounts or services to third parties.

If a key or an account password is published, handed to others or used by many people, we may terminate the paid access without a refund. We will tell you by email to the address given at checkout.

7. Risks we name plainly

The extension works together with EA’s web app on your EA account. The relationship between you and EA is governed by EA’s terms; only you decide whether to use third-party tools on your account, and you check what EA allows. EA may apply measures to accounts at its own discretion, up to a ban. We do not influence EA’s decisions and are not liable for them; the built-in limits reduce the risk but do not remove it. By buying a key you accept this risk and confirm that you have read the terms of use.

EA’s web app changes every September and sometimes mid-season. After a change, some features may not work until we fix them. We post about interruptions on the Telegram channel and add days to the key under clause 5. The term does not depend on a change of game version. If the web app is unavailable to the extension for more than 14 days in a row, the refund policy applies.

The extension takes prices from a third-party site (fut.gg). If it is unavailable, prices may not be shown.

The set of premium features may change together with EA’s web app: features are added, reworked and, where EA has made them impossible, removed. The key gives access to the extension as a whole for its term, not to an unchanging list of features.

10. Data

The extension sends our server only what sign-in and the access check need: the email, the password at sign-in, a random device code and a short device label such as “Windows · Google Chrome”. The password is not stored on the server as itself, only as an irreversible hash. The key is still verified on your computer.

Club lists, purchase journals, the safety counters and the key itself stay on your computer. The extension’s settings travel by two roads, and you switch on both yourself: to our server by the button on the account page, and into Chrome’s own cloud by the button in the settings window. Details are in the privacy policy.

At checkout the payment partner processes your data under its own rules. From it we receive your email and the order amount, so that we can issue access and answer questions.
