1. Words used here

  • Extension: ESHArio, an extension for Google Chrome that works inside EA’s official web app. The extension is installed from our website once, and the code that works inside the web app updates from our server.
  • Premium features: the extension’s features that paid access unlocks. The list is on the buy page.
  • Premium access: the right to use the premium features for the term. It is opened in an account we create for the email given at checkout, or by a licence key.
  • Account: an email and a password. You sign in inside the extension itself, never on an EA page.
  • Key: a string of characters you enter in the extension. The key is signed by us and verified on your computer.
  • Term: 30, 90 or 365 days from the moment access is issued.
  • You: the person who paid for the key. The key may be bought by a person with legal capacity. For a child, the purchase is made in their own name by a parent or other legal representative.

2. What we sell

A simple (non-exclusive) licence: the right to use the extension’s premium features for the term, on your own computers, for yourself. The extension is a tool: everything it does in EA’s web app happens at your instruction, in your name and on your account.

We do not sell game coins, EA accounts, cards or any other in-game items. ESHArio does not promise results in the game.

The set of premium features may change together with EA’s web app. The key gives access to the extension as a whole for its term, not to an unchanging list of features.

The extension itself is free; which features work without a key is set out in the terms of use.

3. How the contract is made

You choose a term on the buy page, enter a promo code if you have one, tick the consent boxes for immediate key delivery and pay on the payment partner’s page.

The contract is made the moment the payment goes through: paying means you accept the offer in full. Nothing needs to be signed: the contract in electronic form has the force of a written one.

Payment is taken by the payment partner in rubles; the payment methods are shown on the payment page. We never see or store your card details.

4. Prices and terms

  • 1 month (30 days): 1,149 ₽.
  • 3 months (90 days): 3,149 ₽.
  • 12 months (365 days): 10,999 ₽.

Prices are in rubles. The price is fixed at the moment of payment; price changes do not affect keys already paid for. If your bank charges you in another currency, the bank sets the rate.

A promo code gives a discount off the price without a code. The size of the discount is shown when you enter the code on the buy page and on the payment page. One promo code per payment.

5. Access delivery and term

Payment goes through the payment partner. As soon as the partner confirms it, we open access in the account for the email given at checkout and write to that address. If there is no account yet, we create one, and the email carries a link for you to set your password; the link works for 24 hours, and a new one can be requested inside the extension with the “Forgot your password” button. If the account already exists, the term is added to it.

A licence key still works as a fallback, on your request. It arrives by email at the same address and is entered in the extension.

Account emails: access issued, a sign-in from a new device, a password change. They carry no advertising.

The term is counted from the moment access is issued, not from the first sign-in.

There is no auto-renewal. Renewal is a new payment. Payment is one-off; there are no repeat charges. If the previous term is still running, its remainder is added to the new one.

If the extension did not work with EA’s web app for longer than a day after a version change of the web app, those days are added to the term.

6. What you may and may not do

You may: sign in and enter the key on your own computers, change computer and browser, use every premium feature during the term.

One account works on two devices at a time. Signing in on a third signs out the device that has been idle longest, and we tell you by email. Keep your password to yourself and do not pass it on.

You may not: transfer, sell or publish the key or give it to other people; modify or disable the extension’s code, its key check or its built-in restrictions; use the extension to sell coins, accounts or services to third parties.

If a key or an account password is published, handed to others or used by many people, we may terminate the paid access without a refund. We will tell you by email to the address given at checkout.

We do not run the payment partner’s infrastructure and are not liable for its failures; this does not limit your rights to receive the key and to a refund.

If access does not work for reasons beyond our control, our obligations are suspended for that time; downtime longer than a day is compensated with days under clause 5.

Nothing in this document limits rights that the law gives you and that cannot be limited by contract.
